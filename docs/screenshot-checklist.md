# Record of the sitting

This checklist replaces a lab screenshot list. Exercise Wharf Lantern has no virtual machine, no console, and no tool output. The evidence that you ran it is a set of photographs or written notes from the room.

Take these during the sitting or immediately after, while the board is still up. Store them as [evidence/README.md](../evidence/README.md) describes. The repository ignores files in `evidence/` other than that README, so a photo is not pushed until you deliberately change that.

Ask before you photograph faces. A photo of the back of a room, of hands, of a board, or of paper is enough. If someone says no, write a note instead and record that a photograph was declined.

## Before you start

- [ ] A photo or a note of the materials on the table: handbook, company brief, blank scribe log, timer. No need for faces.
- [ ] The date, the start time, and whether this is a rehearsal or a facilitated sitting, written on the first page of the scribe log.

## During the sitting

- [ ] The whiteboard or shared document after inject 1, showing roles and the first decision.
- [ ] The same board after inject 4, showing the payment position in the room's words.
- [ ] The three columns if you used them in inject 5: POPIA, Joint Standard, Cybercrimes Act. The value is the words the room used, including "not verified".
- [ ] The customer or holding draft from inject 6, including sentences that were crossed out.
- [ ] The recovery choice from inject 7, including the recovery point the room accepted and what it said clients would lose.
- [ ] One page of the scribe log that shows a dissent, if one happened. If none happened, note that. Do not stage one.

## At the close

- [ ] The list of open actions with owners, photographed or copied.
- [ ] A note that the end-of-sitting check happened: people were asked whether they were leaving with a worry about a real incident. Do not photograph anyone's answer. A "yes, and we spoke afterwards" or "no one raised a concern" line in the scribe log is the record.
- [ ] The stop time.

## If you cannot photograph

Write a one-page note that covers the same bullets. A clear note is better than a dark photo. Sign and date it. That note is the evidence.

## Do not capture

- Faces of people who did not agree.
- Phone numbers, e-mail addresses, or identity documents of real participants.
- A real employer's incident used as an example. If one came up, the facilitator should have parked it. Do not put it in the repository.
- Anything that looks like a payment instruction, a criminal address, or a sample of malware. The injects do not produce those. If a participant drafted one, do not save it. Note that the facilitator stopped the topic.
- Client names. The scenario does not provide any.

## File names

If you later publish a redacted set, name files so a stranger can tell what they show:

- `wharf-lantern-YYYYMMDD-board-inject-04.jpg`
- `wharf-lantern-YYYYMMDD-scribe-log.pdf`
- `wharf-lantern-YYYYMMDD-note.txt`

Keep the original unredacted copies somewhere private, outside this repository.

## What "done" looks like

You can point at a log, a board, and a draft notice, and a reader can see that a room sat with the injects. You cannot point at this checklist alone. An unticked copy only shows that the kit was printed.
