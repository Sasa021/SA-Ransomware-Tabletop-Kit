# Scribe log

Exercise Wharf Lantern. One row is one moment that matters. You are not writing a transcript.

Rules for the scribe:

- Write what was said that changed a fact, a decision, or an open question. Leave out jokes and repetition.
- Put words you are unsure of in quotation marks and mark them "check".
- Do not improve a decision. If the room said "no decision", write "no decision".
- Record dissent in the same row as the decision. A dissent that is only in your memory did not happen, for the purposes of the after action report.
- Separate the scenario clock (Monday 07:40 and so on) from the exercise clock (minutes since the sitting started).
- Do not add a client name, an identity number, a payment method, or a real person's contact detail. The injects do not contain them.
- You may stop the room and say the current decision is not clear enough to log.
