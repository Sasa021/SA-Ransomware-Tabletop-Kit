# Inject 8. Close

**Scenario time:** Friday 09:00
**Exercise aim:** Record what is still open, who owns it, and what the firm will say when a regulator asks for an update. Then stop. This inject is not a results page.

## Read this aloud

It is Friday. Use the recovery decision the room actually made. If the room did not make one, BaySwitch did nothing overnight, and Friday's reporting pack does not exist.

Two messages arrive.

The Financial Sector Conduct Authority, using the contact path Harbourline used if the room reported a material incident, asks for a short update: what is restored, what clients have been told, and what evidence you are keeping. If the room never classified the incident and never notified the Authority, the message does not arrive. Instead, Thabo reads paragraph 9 of Joint Standard 2 of 2024 again and asks whether silence is still a defensible position on Friday.

A client emails Johan to say they will move their portfolio next month. They cite "the silence", not a published file. No file has been published in this scenario unless your own room claimed one was. There is no published file in the injects.

Dr September wants a five-line brief she can read to the board this afternoon. She wants decisions, owners, and dates. She does not want a story about whose fault the Thursday attachment was.

## Facts the room may use

- The only new external pressure is a request for an update, and only if you notified the Authority.
- One client is leaving because communication failed, in their view.
- Nothing in the official injects says the stolen files were published or that a payment succeeded.

## What you do not know yet

- The forensic answers. They are out of scope. The after action report may list them as open questions.
- How the board will react. You are writing the brief, not the minutes of a meeting you did not hold.

## Close the exercise

Stop the scenario clock. Thank the scribe. Read back the decisions. Ask the check in the facilitator guide: is anyone carrying a worry about a real incident. Then fill the hot wash prompts in [inject section of the facilitator guide](../facilitator-guide.md). Leave [the results section of the README](../README.md) for what this room actually did.
