# Inject 1. Detection

**Scenario time:** Monday 07:40
**Exercise aim:** Stand up roles, contain speculation, and decide the next hour.

## Read this aloud

It is Monday 07:40. Two advisers are already at their desks on the Foreshore. Client folders on QUAY-FS01 will not open. The file names are still there. A short note has replaced the desktop background on both PCs. The note says the files are encrypted and that Harbourline must make contact if it wants them back. It does not, in this exercise, give a payment method, an amount, or an address. Do not invent one.

Lerato Jacobs is on the bus and ten minutes away. She has seen a photo of the screen on the staff WhatsApp group. Email still works. LedgerQuay still opens for the one adviser who tried it. The client portal has not been checked. Johan Pietersen is making coffee and has not seen the note. Amina Fourie is in the office. Thabo Meyer is not in yet. BaySwitch has not been called.

## Facts the room may use

- Two PCs show the same note.
- Shared folders on QUAY-FS01 do not open.
- LedgerQuay opened once, a few minutes ago.
- Nobody has isolated a machine, a server, or the office network.
- The WhatsApp photo is already on a group that includes people who are not in the office.

## What you do not know yet

- Whether this is one shared folder or the whole server.
- Whether any personal information has been copied, or only encrypted.
- Whether email, the portal, or LedgerQuay will still be safe in an hour.
- Who, if anyone, opened a malicious file. Do not guess a person.
