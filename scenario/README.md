# Scenario: Exercise Wharf Lantern

Exercise Wharf Lantern is a ransomware tabletop at Harbourline Discretionary (Pty) Ltd, a fictional discretionary financial services provider on the Foreshore in Cape Town.

The scenario clock starts at 07:40 on a Monday. That clock is the story. The exercise clock in the facilitator guide is the timer in the room. Keep them separate when you take notes. Write "scenario 07:40" or "exercise minute 15" so the after action report does not mix them.

## Read order

Hand out [company-brief.md](company-brief.md) at the start. Release the injects one at a time. Do not print them as one bundle for participants.

| Order | File | Scenario time | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [inject-01-detection.md](inject-01-detection.md) | Monday 07:40 | Files will not open. A note is on the screens. |
| 2 | [inject-02-spread-and-backups.md](inject-02-spread-and-backups.md) | Monday 08:25 | More machines are affected. One backup set looks unreadable. An older copy is offline. |
| 3 | [inject-03-accounts.md](inject-03-accounts.md) | Monday 09:40 | A privileged sign-in and a new administrator account. The client portal is down. |
| 4 | [inject-04-extortion.md](inject-04-extortion.md) | Monday 11:40 | A claim that client files will be published if the firm does not pay. |
| 5 | [inject-05-authorities.md](inject-05-authorities.md) | Monday 13:40 | Counsel asks who must be told, and what must be kept. |
| 6 | [inject-06-comms-pressure.md](inject-06-comms-pressure.md) | Tuesday 08:10 | Staff rumours, client calls, and a journalist. |
| 7 | [inject-07-recovery.md](inject-07-recovery.md) | Tuesday 15:00 | Two backup choices and a Friday reporting deadline. |
| 8 | [inject-08-close.md](inject-08-close.md) | Friday 09:00 | The regulator asks for an update. The room closes the exercise. |

[mitre-map.md](mitre-map.md) lists the MITRE ATT&CK techniques a defender can name from these facts. It is a learning aid for the facilitator. It is not a procedure, and it does not say the attribution is proven inside the story. Several techniques are marked unconfirmed because the room is not given forensic proof.

The injects describe what staff and the managed service provider report. They do not include commands, samples of malware, payment instructions, or addresses for contacting a criminal. If a participant goes looking for those during the sitting, stop that line of talk and bring the room back to the decision in front of it.
