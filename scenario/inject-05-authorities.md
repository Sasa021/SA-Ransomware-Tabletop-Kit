# Inject 5. SAPS and the regulators

**Scenario time:** Monday 13:40
**Exercise aim:** Decide who must be told, who may be told, and what must be preserved. Separate the three legal paths.

## Read this aloud

Amina has reached external counsel on a short call. Counsel is not in the room and will not give you a ruling. Counsel asks the room to come back with four answers: whether there are reasonable grounds to believe personal information was accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person; whether Harbourline will classify this as a material incident for the Financial Sector Conduct Authority; whether anyone has spoken to the South African Police Service; and what has been preserved.

Thabo has POPIA section 22 open. He reminds the room that Harbourline is the responsible party, and that BaySwitch is an operator. BaySwitch says they mailed Lerato at 08:40 with the words "client data may be involved". Lerato read it and did not forward it to Amina or Thabo.

The detective question is open. Nobody has a case number. The section 54 question is also open. Read the breach notification guide before you let the room invent a 72 hour duty or a 24 hour duty. The guide explains which clocks are in a statute, which sit in a later notice, and which are not yet in force.

Johan wants to send clients a reassurance that "no data was stolen" before the close of business, so that the property transfer does not fall over.

## Facts the room may use

- Counsel wants four answers, not a press release.
- BaySwitch told Lerato at 08:40 that client data may be involved. The Information Officer does not have that sentence yet unless the room passes it on.
- No case number exists.
- A client-facing sentence that says "no data was stolen" would go beyond the facts.
- The legal paths are written up in [../regulatory/breach-notification-guide.md](../regulatory/breach-notification-guide.md). Use that guide. Do not invent a deadline it refuses to state.

## What you do not know yet

- Whether a public body or the Information Regulator has determined that telling data subjects would impede a criminal investigation. Until that determination exists, section 22(3) does not give the firm a private right to stay silent.
- Whether the Minister has prescribed the categories and the form for Cybercrimes Act section 54. The guide tells you to check, and not to assume.

## Reveal after the discussion

No new attacker technique. The techniques from injects 1 to 4 are the ones a report would name, with the same "confirmed" and "suspected" labels.

What the room should be able to say out loud:

- POPIA section 22 is about personal information and the Information Regulator and data subjects.
- Joint Standard 2 of 2024 paragraph 9 is about a material cyber incident or information security compromise and the responsible authority.
- Cybercrimes Act section 54 is a reporting duty aimed at electronic communications service providers and financial institutions, and it has a commencement problem the guide describes.
