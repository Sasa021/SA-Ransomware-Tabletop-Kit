# Inject 6. Staff, clients, and a journalist

**Scenario time:** Tuesday 08:10
**Exercise aim:** Say only what the room knows, to the people who need to hear it, in a channel that reaches them.

## Read this aloud

It is Tuesday morning. The office is open. QUAY-FS01 is still unreadable. LedgerQuay is still down. Advisers are taking notes on paper.

Three new facts:

1. The staff WhatsApp group, which includes two contractors who left last month, has a thread speculating that "Amina will pay tonight" and naming a client whose identity document was in a shared folder. The client is not named in this inject. Do not invent the name.
2. Johan has 27 missed calls. The family with the property transfer has emailed to say the transferring attorney will pause unless Harbourline confirms the balance in writing by noon.
3. A journalist at **Peninsula Business Daily**, a fictional Cape Town newsroom, emails info@harbourline.example. The subject line is "Client data for sale?" The body asks whether Harbourline has been hit by ransomware, whether clients should freeze their bank accounts, and whether the firm will comment before a story runs this afternoon. The journalist does not claim to have a document. Do not invent one.

Dr September has still not had a full brief. She has seen a screenshot from the WhatsApp group, forwarded by a friend of a staff member.

The communications templates in [../comms/crisis-communications-plan.md](../comms/crisis-communications-plan.md) are available. Fill only the brackets you can support from the brief and the injects.

## Facts the room may use

- Rumours have left the employed group.
- A real transaction in the story, the property transfer, needs a balance by noon. The balance system is down.
- A journalist is asking three questions and has not produced a document.
- The board chair is hearing the story from outside the firm.

## What you do not know yet

- Whether any client file has been published.
- Whether bank accounts of clients are at risk of fraud. You know account numbers are in the files. You do not know they have been misused.
- What Dr September has already said to her friend.

## Reveal after the discussion

No new technique ID. If the room wants a label for the publication threat, keep it as the unconfirmed extortion from inject 4. A news story is not a MITRE technique.
