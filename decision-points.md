# Decision points

Questions for the facilitator to ask during Exercise Wharf Lantern. Do not send this file to participants before the sitting. Pick the questions the room is avoiding. You do not need to ask every line.

There is no answer key. Record the room's answer in the scribe log. If the room will not choose, record "no decision" and what it would cost to wait.

Where a question touches a legal duty, the longer note is [regulatory/breach-notification-guide.md](regulatory/breach-notification-guide.md). Do not let these questions invent a deadline that guide refuses to state.

## Inject 1. Detection (Monday 07:40)

1. Who is the incident lead for the next hour, and who is the scribe?
2. What do you protect first: the two PCs, the file server, LedgerQuay, or email? What are you willing to switch off before Lerato arrives?
3. The photo is already on WhatsApp. What do you tell staff in the next message, and what do you tell them not to forward?
4. Do you cancel the 09:00 meeting now, or wait until you know more? Who tells the advisers?
5. What facts would move this from "IT fault" to "possible security compromise" in your own mind? You do not have all of them yet. Say which ones are missing.

## Inject 2. Spread and backups (Monday 08:25)

1. The laptop in Stellenbosch is still online. Do you order it switched off, disconnected, or left on so someone can look at it? Who gives that order?
2. The on-network backup is unreadable. Do you treat that as part of the incident or as a separate failure? What changes in your next action either way?
3. Will you ask Salt River Vault Storage to move the offline set, or will you leave it untouched until you know you have a clean place to restore it?
4. The Thursday attachment is a lead, not a finding. What do you do with it that does not depend on it being the cause?
5. Amina wants the investment meeting to go ahead so staff are not alarmed. What is the cost of that reassurance if the server is still being written to?

## Inject 3. Accounts and the portal (Monday 09:40)

1. BaySwitch is waiting for written permission to disable the privileged account and the new administrator account. Do you give it, narrow it, or withhold it? Write the sentence you would actually send.
2. Do you isolate the office network, and what client service dies the moment you do?
3. Clients are asking for balances for a property transfer today. What can Johan say that is true?
4. What do you tell Dr September in a three-minute call? List the three facts you will include and one fact you will not imply.
5. Which MITRE labels are earned by a sign-in and a new account, and which ones are still a theory? Use the map only after you have answered in your own words.

## Inject 4. Extortion: pay or not pay (Monday 11:40)

The room may decide to refuse, to defer, or to authorise counsel and the bank to examine whether any payment can even be considered. The room may not design a payment.

1. What outcome would a payment be trying to buy: a key, a promise not to publish, both, or time? How would you know you had received it?
2. What remains true if you pay? You still do not have proof that copies were deleted. You still have a POPIA question. You still have an untested backup.
3. What remains true if you do not pay? You still have option A and option B in inject 7. You still have clients whose information may be in someone else's hands.
4. Who, on Harbourline's facts, is allowed to instruct the bank? What will you ask counsel before that instruction exists?
5. There is no cyber insurance. Does that change the decision, or only the funding?
6. Will you ask for the "sample" the message offers? What harm could that request cause, and who decides?
7. If a participant starts to explain how to move money or how to reach the sender, stop. The decision on the table is authority and purpose, not a method.

## Inject 5. Law enforcement and regulators (Monday 13:40)

Keep three columns: POPIA, the Joint Standard, and the Cybercrimes Act.

1. Are there reasonable grounds to believe personal information was accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person? Which facts support that, and which facts are only a criminal's claim?
2. BaySwitch told Lerato at 08:40 and Amina still does not know. What should an operator's call path be, today, for the rest of this incident?
3. Do you notify the Information Regulator on the information you have, or do you wait for proof? Read the guide before you answer. Section 22 does not give you a fixed hour. It also does not tell you to finish the investigation first.
4. Can you delay telling clients because a detective might prefer silence? Only if a public body responsible for the investigation, or the Regulator, determines that notification will impede a criminal investigation. Has anyone made that determination in this story?
5. Is this a material incident under Joint Standard 2 of 2024? Use the definition: severe and widespread impact on the firm's operations, its services to customers, or the broader financial system and economy. A small FSP can meet that definition through its own clients without being a threat to the financial system. Say which limb you are using. If you decide it is not material, say what would change your mind, and who will reassess.
6. If you do classify it as material, which authority do you notify, through which portal, and what period will you check on the template cover page before you quote a number of hours?
7. Cybercrimes Act section 54 names electronic communications service providers and financial institutions. Harbourline is set up so the question arises. Before you start a 72 hour clock, answer three checks from the guide: is the section in force today, is this offence in a category the Minister has prescribed, and has the form and manner been prescribed? If any answer is "we have not checked", you do not have a clock yet. You may still report a crime to SAPS.
8. What will you preserve, where will it be stored, and who is allowed to open it? Include the firewall summary, the quarantine from Friday, and the image of the note.
9. Johan wants to tell clients that no data was stolen. What is wrong with that sentence on these facts, and what sentence replaces it?

## Inject 6. Customer and staff communications (Tuesday 08:10)

Use the templates in the crisis communications plan. Fill brackets only from known facts.

1. What does the internal message need to stop in the next hour? The WhatsApp thread is the constraint. Who is on that group and should not be?
2. The attorney needs something by noon. What can you confirm about the balance, and what alternative do you offer that does not invent a figure?
3. Do you send a customer notice now, a holding line, or nothing until the Information Officer signs a section 22 notice? Who is the audience for each?
4. The journalist asked three questions. Which will you answer, which will you decline, and who is the only person allowed to send the reply?
5. Dr September heard a rumour before she heard from you. What do you send her in the next 15 minutes of scenario time?
6. Which stakeholder on the map has not been considered? Look at staff families, the storage provider, and the panel attorney, not only at the press.
7. Read your draft aloud and underline every clause that is stronger than the facts. Cut those clauses.

## Inject 7. Backups and recovery (Tuesday 15:00)

1. Do you bring the 11 day offline set back, leave it sealed, or open it only on a machine that is not joined to the office network?
2. Do you allow BaySwitch to restore the Sunday cloud copy onto a separate network for inspection? What would make you refuse to promote it to the live system?
3. The firm never set a recovery point objective. Set one now, in days of data you are willing to lose, and say which clients carry that loss.
4. Email still has some withdrawal instructions. Is email a trustworthy source of those instructions, given the rest of the week? What check do you want before Johan acts on one?
5. Name the condition for switching the client portal back on. "When IT says it is fine" is not a condition.
6. Paying, if you chose it on Monday, has not produced a key in this story. What is your recovery plan with that fact held still?

## Inject 8. Close (Friday 09:00)

1. List open actions with an owner and a date. If there is no date, the action is a wish.
2. If you notified the Financial Sector Conduct Authority, what three sentences answer the update request? If you did not, is silence still defensible on Friday, and who reopens the classification?
3. One client is leaving because of the silence. What, if anything, do you owe that client beyond the section 22 content?
4. What will the five-line board brief say about payment, reporting, backups, and communications? Five lines, not a narrative.
5. What will you not write in the after action report because the room never decided it?

## Across the whole exercise

Use these in the hot wash if you have five minutes.

- Where did the first story you liked survive after the facts got worse?
- Where did rank, or the wish not to alarm people, delay a containment step?
- Which single decision would you defend to Dr September without adding new facts?
